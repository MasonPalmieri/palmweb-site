Process

How it works.

Four short stages from first call to live site.

Step 01

Discovery Call

We talk through your goals, audience, and budget. No commitment — just a clear picture of what you need.

Step 02

Design & Build

I design and code your site from scratch. You see progress along the way and give feedback in real time.

Step 03

Review & Refine

Two rounds of revisions are included. We make sure everything looks right and works before it goes live.

Step 04

Launch & Support

Your site goes live. I handle hosting setup, domain config, and any questions that come up after launch.
