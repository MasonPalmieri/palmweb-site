Our approach

A revenue cycle you don't have to manage.

Point-of-care dispensing works when the operational lift is invisible. CCRx handles the moving pieces — from product management through claim resolution — so your clinic delivers a better patient experience without adding staff or workflow complexity.

  • Product management

    Ordering, inventory, and replenishment for Rx and OTC pain management products.

  • Billing & collections

    End-to-end submission and follow-through, tuned for workers' compensation and PIP/MVA claims.

  • Claim resolution

    Denials, appeals, and payer communication — so revenue is captured, not written off.

  • Concierge support

    A dedicated team that acts as an extension of your practice, not another vendor to manage.
