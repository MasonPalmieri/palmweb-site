Who we serve

One partner. Three aligned outcomes.

01

For the patient

A drug-free, non-narcotic, and non-surgical alternative for managing pain following a workplace injury — delivered at the point of care for a smoother recovery and higher satisfaction.

02

For the practice

Better patient outcomes and a single point of care, paired with a new ancillary revenue stream — without the headache of owning the entire dispensing and billing process.

03

The CCRx role

A concierge support team managing product logistics, billing, collections, and claim resolution end-to-end. We streamline operations, minimize administrative burden, and improve financial performance for the clinics we serve.

Our approach

A revenue cycle you don't have to manage.

Point-of-care dispensing works when the operational lift is invisible. CCRx handles the moving pieces — from product management through claim resolution — so your clinic delivers a better patient experience without adding staff or workflow complexity.

  • Product management

    Ordering, inventory, and replenishment for Rx and OTC pain management products.

  • Billing & collections

    End-to-end submission and follow-through, tuned for workers' compensation and PIP/MVA claims.

  • Claim resolution

    Denials, appeals, and payer communication — so revenue is captured, not written off.

  • Concierge support

    A dedicated team that acts as an extension of your practice, not another vendor to manage.
