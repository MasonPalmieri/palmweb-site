What we do

One partnership. Three people it serves.

— 01

The patient recovers with fewer trade-offs.

An opioid-sparing, non-surgical option for managing pain after a workers' compensation or personal injury/MVA claim — dispensed at the same visit, without extra pharmacy trips. Patients feel cared for, and satisfaction improves.

— 02

The practice gains a new avenue of care.

Better outcomes and a single, consistent care experience for the patient, paired with an ancillary revenue stream — without the operational headache of owning the dispensing, billing, or claim workflow.

— 03

CCRx quietly runs the operations.

Our physician participation pharmacy platform manages product, billing, collections, and claim resolution end-to-end. We minimize administrative overhead, improve financial performance, and let the clinical team stay focused on the patient in front of them.
