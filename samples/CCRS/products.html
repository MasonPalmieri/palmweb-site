NICE1
NICE1 — Cold + Compression

The smallest, lightest cold therapy device on the market.

An iceless, programmable cold & compression console that goes wherever recovery does.

  • 8×8×8 in form factor, just 9 lbs
  • Completely iceless — no ice, no mess, no nonsense
  • Time- and temperature-controlled (42°F to 58°F)
  • Promotes opioid reduction via immediate, localized pain relief
  • Supports hospital-to-home continuity of care

ambIT
Electronic Pain System

ambIT — programmed, intermittent pain control.

An electronic pump that delivers intermittent medication based on a programmed rate. Bolus local anesthetic to manage breakthrough pain — proven to reduce opioid use, shorten hospital stays, and reduce pain.

  • Suitable for pediatric and adult patients
  • Programmable bolus + continuous delivery profiles
  • Compact, ambulatory form factor
PF0001
DVT Prevention

PlasmaFlow Sequential Compression

A wireless, lightweight sequential compression system designed to help prevent the onset of deep-vein thrombosis (DVT) by stimulating blood flow in the extremities.

DJOBGS
Bone Growth Stimulators

DJO CMF OL1000 & Spinalogic

Portable, battery-powered, noninvasive bone growth stimulators. CMF OL1000 is indicated for established nonunion fractures (excluding vertebrae and flat bones); Spinalogic is an adjunct to primary lumbar fusion at one or two levels.
